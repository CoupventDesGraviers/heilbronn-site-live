About the structure

The 16 points have rational coordinates (denominators 31 and 33), 180-degree rotational symmetry, and exactly 64 triangles tied at area 7/341. Eight axis-aligned tesseract faces each contain 4 minimal triangles; six diagonal parallelograms contribute the remaining 24 among the 64.
